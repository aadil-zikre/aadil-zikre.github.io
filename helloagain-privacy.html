Hello Again Old Friend — Privacy Policy

Last updated August 6, 2026

Hello Again Old Friend is a personal app that helps you stay in touch with people you care about. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, name, and timezone when you create an account.
  • Contact information about people you add — the name, email, and (optionally) phone number of anyone you choose to add to your list. This is information about people who are not themselves users of Hello Again Old Friend.
  • Anecdotes and notes — the free-text notes you write about the people you add, including their dates and categories (some categories, like health or bereavement, are treated as sensitive within the app).
  • Message drafts and history — drafts generated for you, any edits you make, and a log of when you reached out to someone.

How we use this information

Everything above exists to run the app's core feature: reminding you to reach out to people you care about, and helping you draft something specific and warm rather than generic. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't build profiles for any purpose beyond making this app work for you.

AI-assisted drafts

Message drafts are generated by an open-source language model that runs on our own server — your anecdotes and draft content are not sent to any third-party AI company to generate a draft.

Data sharing

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data is stored on our infrastructure provider's servers, used solely to run the app.

Your control over your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it — every person, anecdote, cadence, and draft — with no waiting period.

Children's privacy

Hello Again Old Friend is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
